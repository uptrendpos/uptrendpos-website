FAQ

Questions about our prices

How much does UpTrendPos cost?

Our POS software pricing starts at Rs 2,000 a month for Premium. Enterprise is Rs 3,000 a month and Custom is Rs 6,000 a month. All plans have unlimited invoices.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new shop gets a 14-day free trial. Add your products, make sales and see if it fits before you pay.

Is there a free plan?

No. We keep one clear price for each plan instead. Use the 14-day trial to test everything first.

Is there an installation fee?

Yes. You pay a one-time installation fee when you buy a plan: Rs 20,000 for Premium, Rs 30,000 for Enterprise and Rs 60,000 for Custom. It covers account setup, help loading your items and staff training. After that you only pay the monthly or yearly plan price.

Do I save money if I pay yearly?

Yes. Pay for a full year and get 10% off the first year. For example, Premium is Rs 21,600 for the first year instead of Rs 24,000.

What is a branch and what is a user?

A branch is one shop location with its own stock and sales. A user is one staff login, like a cashier or manager. Premium has 1 branch and 3 users, Enterprise has 2 branches and 6 users, and Custom has 3 branches and 9 users.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a bigger plan any time. Your products, sales and accounts stay as they are.

Which plan has FBR integration?

FBR invoices with QR code come with Enterprise and Custom. Premium does not include FBR.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, on every plan. The Windows and Android apps keep billing offline and sync when the internet comes back.

Do I need to buy new hardware?

No. Use your own PC, laptop, Android tablet or phone. Add a USB barcode scanner and a thermal printer if you want faster billing.
