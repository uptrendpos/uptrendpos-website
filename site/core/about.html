Does UpTrendPos work without internet?

Yes. The Windows and Android apps keep billing offline during load-shedding or a weak signal. Every sale is saved on your device first and syncs to your account when the internet comes back, with no double entries. You need internet only for the first login.

Is UpTrendPos integrated with FBR?

Yes. Shops registered with FBR as Tier-1 retailers can add their FBR POS ID, and each bill then shows an FBR invoice number and QR code. See how FBR POS integration works or the official FBR website.

Which devices does it run on?

UpTrendPos runs on Windows computers, Android phones and tablets, and in any web browser, including on a Mac or iPhone. One account works on all your devices, so the owner can check sales from the phone while staff bill on the counter PC. See the download page.
