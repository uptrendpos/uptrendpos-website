FAQ

Download questions

Is it free to download POS software from UpTrendPos?

Yes. Downloading the Windows and Android apps is free, and every new shop gets a 14-day free trial. After the trial, choose a plan on our pricing page.

Which version should I choose?

Use the Windows app on your shop counter computer, the Android app on your phone, and the web app on any other laptop, Mac or tablet. You can download POS software for both Windows and Android and use all of them with one account.

Do I need internet to use it?

You need internet for the first login. After that, the Windows and Android apps keep billing offline and sync your data when the internet comes back.

Can I use the same account on my PC and my phone?

Yes. Log in with the same account on every device. Sales, stock and udhaar sync between them automatically.

Is the APK safe to install?

Yes, when you download it from this page. Android shows a warning for any app installed outside the Play Store. Never install an UpTrendPos file sent from another website or an unknown number.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. On iPhone and iPad, open live.uptrendpos.com in Safari. It works with the same account.

How do I update the app?

Download POS software updates from this page and install the new version over the old one. Your data is saved in your account, so it stays safe. Make sure all sales are synced before you update.

Which shops can use it?

UpTrendPos has ready setups for 30+ types of shops, from grocery and pharmacy to clothing and mobile shops. See all business types or the features on our home page.
