Can I try the software myself?

Yes. Start the 14-day free trial on the web and test billing, stock and udhaar with your own products. You can still ask us for a demo or setup help during the trial.

What do I need for FBR setup?

FBR invoices come with the Enterprise and Custom plans. Keep your NTN or STRN, POS registration number, POS terminal ID and API token ready. We help you add them and test your first FBR invoice.

Which printer and scanner should I buy?

Most shops use an 80mm thermal receipt printer and a USB barcode scanner. Send us a WhatsApp message before you buy and we will tell you what works best with UpTrendPos for your counter.

Do you give support after I buy?

Yes. After setup, you can message or call our team in Urdu or English when you need help. We also train your staff on billing, returns and udhaar so your counter runs smoothly.
