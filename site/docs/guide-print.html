Glossary

Words used in the app

Udhaar / khata
Goods given on credit, and the customer ledger that keeps the balance.
Qist
An installment. A big sale paid in parts, often monthly.
PO
Purchase order. What you plan to buy from a vendor.
GRN
Goods received note. Made when stock arrives; it adds the stock.
PI
Purchase invoice. The vendor bill made from the GRN.
Reverse GRN
Sends stock back to the vendor and corrects stock and the vendor balance.
UOM
Unit of measure: piece, pack, carton, kg, metre and so on.
Loose sale
Selling single units out of a pack, like one tablet or one biscuit packet.
Cash session
Opening and closing a cash drawer for one shift, with the count.
Manager PIN
A code only the manager knows, needed to approve risky changes.
CPV / CRV
Cash payment voucher / cash receipt voucher.
BPV / BRV
Bank payment voucher / bank receipt voucher.
CV (contra)
Moving money between your own cash and bank accounts.
JV
Journal voucher, for adjustments that are not cash or bank.
Day end sweep
The end-of-day step that moves the day's cash to the right accounts.
Period lock
Closes past days so nobody can change them.
Year-end close
Closes the financial year and carries balances forward.
Held postings
Entries waiting for approval before they reach the accounts.
Markup income
The extra earned on installment sales, counted as it is earned.
AR aging
Who owes you money and for how long.
Trial balance
A check that all debits equal all credits in your accounts.
FBR fiscalization
Sending each bill to FBR so it gets an FBR invoice number and QR code.
