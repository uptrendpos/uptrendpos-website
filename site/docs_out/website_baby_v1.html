    Sell a full pack or loose pieces. Stock is kept in pieces, so it always adds up.

    Every diaper size has its own stock, with low stock alerts and a demand list.

FAQ

Baby store POS software questions

Which is the best baby store POS software in Pakistan?

The best baby store POS software is the one built for packs and sizes, not just bills. Check five things: pack and piece selling, stock for every diaper size, batch and expiry alerts, barcode billing and offline billing. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Can I sell diapers by the pack and by the piece?

Yes. Set the pack size once, such as 40 diapers. Sell a full pack or 5 loose pieces on the same bill. Stock is counted in pieces, so packs and loose pieces always add up.

Can I keep stock for every diaper size?

Yes. Each size is its own item with its own stock and price. The variant generator makes all sizes of a product in one go, and low stock alerts show which sizes to reorder.

How does this diaper shop software track expiry?

Enter the batch and expiry date when baby food, formula or wipes come in. The dashboard shows expiring and expired items, and the batch-wise report lists every batch with its date. Sell the oldest stock first, or return it to the supplier before it becomes a loss.

Do you have a ready list of baby products?

No. Your shop starts with an empty list and you add the items you sell. Most baby products have a barcode, so scanning makes setup and billing fast.

Can I sell baby clothes and toys too?

Yes. Baby clothes can be made by age size with the variant generator, and toys, gifts and bath items sell as normal items on the same bill.

How do I handle damaged packs?

Record torn or damaged packs as damage. The stock goes down and the cost is recorded, so your stock count and profit stay right.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

Can I give regular families udhaar?

Yes. Every customer can have a khata. Credit sales and payments go on the ledger, and you can send the statement on WhatsApp.

How much does baby store POS software cost?

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Add counters with separate staff logins and cash sessions, and each branch keeps its own stock and batches. The owner sees every branch from one account. Limits depend on your plan.
