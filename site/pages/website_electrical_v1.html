The problems

Common problems electrical shop owners face without POS software

An electrical shop buys in coils, boxes and cartons but sells in meters and pieces, often on credit to electricians. A register cannot follow this. Good electrical store POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Wire stock never matches

    You buy a 90 meter coil and sell 15 meters here, 7 meters there. Nobody knows how many meters are left.

    Buy by the coil, sell by the meter. Stock drops in meters on every bill.

  • The problem

    Open boxes, lost pieces

    Switches, sockets and bulbs come in boxes but sell one by one. Loose pieces are never counted.

    Set the box size once. Sell by the piece or the full box, and stock stays right.

  • The problem

    Quotes written on paper

    An electrician asks for a full house wiring price. The paper quote gets lost before he comes back.

    Make an A4 quote, send it on WhatsApp and turn it into a sale in one tap.

  • The problem

    Electricians' udhaar in a diary

    Regular electricians take goods on credit all month, and the diary never adds up.

    Udhaar khata for every electrician and contractor, with a statement on WhatsApp.

  • The problem

    Fused and broken items

    Fused bulbs and broken fittings are thrown away, but stock still shows them.

    Record them as damage. Stock and cost stay right.

  • The problem

    Many models of one LED

    Same bulb in 9W, 12W and 18W, cool and warm. The shelf and the register never match.

    The variant generator makes every watt and colour as its own item with its own stock.

Electrical store POS software questions

Which is the best electrical store POS software in Pakistan?

The best electrical store POS software is the one built for coils and boxes, not groceries. Check five things: wire by the meter, box to piece selling, A4 quotes, udhaar for electricians and offline billing. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Can I buy wire by the coil and sell it by the meter?

Yes. Set the coil size once, such as 90 meters. When you sell 15 meters, the stock drops by 15 meters, and the cost per meter is worked out for you.

Can I sell switches and bulbs from a box one by one?

Yes. Set the box or carton size once. Sell by the piece or the full box, and the stock always shows the right number of pieces.

Can I give electricians a quotation?

Yes. Make an A4 quotation with your logo and send it on WhatsApp. When the electrician comes back, convert it to a sale in one tap.

Does this electrical shop software keep udhaar for electricians?

Yes. Every electrician or contractor gets a khata. Credit sales and payments go on the ledger, and you can send the statement on WhatsApp.

Do you have a ready list of electrical items?

No. Your shop starts with an empty list and you add the items you sell. The variant generator makes every watt and colour of an LED model in one go.

How do I handle fused bulbs and broken fittings?

Record them as damage. The stock goes down and the cost is recorded, so your stock count and profit stay right. Items that go back to the supplier are recorded as a return.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.
