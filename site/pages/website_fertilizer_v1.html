The problems

Common problems agri dealers face without POS software

An agri inputs shop is busy for a few weeks at sowing time and quiet for the rest. Farmers buy on udhaar and pay after the harvest, sprays and seed have an expiry date, and fertilizer moves in bags by the truck. A register and a calculator cannot keep up. Good fertilizer shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Udhaar for a whole season

    Farmers buy seed in sowing time, fertilizer a month later and sprays after that. They pay after the harvest, and the register has six months of cuttings.

    Every sale on the customer ledger with every payment. Send a clear statement on WhatsApp before the harvest money comes in.

  • The problem

    Sprays expire on the shelf

    A carton of spray sits at the back of the store. When you find it, the date has passed and the money is gone.

    Batch number and expiry date on every purchase. Expiring and expired alerts tell you what to sell first or return.

  • The problem

    Bags and kg mixed up

    You buy fertilizer in full bags but some farmers want 10 kg or 25 kg. The stock count turns into a guess.

    Units: sell by the bag or by the kg, sprays by the bottle or carton. Stock is always kept in the unit you sell.

  • The problem

    Sowing-time rush

    At sowing time farmers stand at the counter in a line. Writing each bill by hand takes too long and mistakes creep in.

    Quick billing: scan a barcode or search by name, add bag and bottle items, and print or send the bill in seconds.

  • The problem

    Supplier balance unclear

    You take stock from many dealers and companies. Nobody knows what is still due to each supplier and which bill is old.

    Purchase invoice, goods received (GRN) and a supplier ledger with the balance due and how old each bill is.

  • The problem

    Load-shedding stops the counter

    The light goes, the internet goes, and a farmer from the village is waiting with cash in hand.

    Our fertilizer shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Fertilizer shop POS software questions

Which is the best fertilizer shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best fertilizer shop POS software is the one built for the way an agri shop works. Check five things: bag and kg units, batch and expiry alerts for sprays and seed, udhaar for farmers till the harvest, supplier ledgers, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 7 days.

Can I sell fertilizer by the bag and by the kg?

Yes. Set the units for each item once. Sell a full bag or loose kg, and stock always stays in the selling unit. Sprays can sell by the bottle or carton and seed by the packet or kg, all on one bill.

How do expiry alerts work for sprays and seed?

Enter the batch number and expiry date when stock comes in. UpTrendPos shows expiring and expired batches, and the batch-wise stock and expiry report shows what to sell first or return to the supplier.

Can farmers buy on udhaar for the whole season?

Yes. Every unpaid sale goes on the farmer's customer ledger, and every payment sits on the same ledger. When the crop is sold, send the statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp and collect the balance.

Can a farmer pay in parts after the harvest?

Yes. Receive any amount at any time on the customer ledger, and the balance updates on its own. On Enterprise and Custom plans you can also set qist plans with a guarantor.

Do you have a ready list of seed, fertilizer and pesticide items?

No. Your shop starts with an empty list and you add only what you sell. The variant generator makes, for example, every pack size of one spray in one go. You can also add products from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products by name or category.

Can I return expired or damaged stock to the supplier?

Yes. Make a return to the supplier, and stock and the supplier balance update on their own. Stock that cannot go back is recorded as damage, so your stock stays true.

Does it give advice on which spray or fertilizer to use?

No. UpTrendPos is software for billing, stock, udhaar and accounts. It does not give crop or spray advice.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar and qist need internet so every device shows the same balance.

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Add counters with separate staff logins and cash sessions, and each branch keeps its own stock. The owner sees every branch from one account. Limits depend on your plan.
