The problems

Common problems furniture shop owners face without POS software

A furniture shop sells costly items, gives quotes before the sale, takes advances and often sells on qist. A register and a receipt book cannot keep all of this straight. Good furniture shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Quotes on a notepad

    A family asks for the price of a full bedroom set. You write it on a notepad, and next week nobody remembers the price you gave.

    Make an A4 quote with your logo, send it on WhatsApp and turn it into a sale in one tap.

  • The problem

    Advances on a slip

    A customer pays Rs 50,000 advance for a sofa set. The slip is lost, and at delivery there is an argument about how much was paid.

    Record the advance on the customer ledger. The bill and every payment sit on one ledger, so the balance is clear.

  • The problem

    Which finish is in the showroom?

    The same bed comes in walnut, teak and white. You do not know which finish is in stock without walking to the godown.

    Every model in every finish or colour is its own item with its own stock.

  • The problem

    Qist in a register

    Customers buy on monthly installments, and the qist register never matches what was collected.

    Qist plans with a guarantor on Enterprise and Custom, with each installment on the record.

  • The problem

    Big balances, no statement

    Customers owe large amounts after delivery, and you have no clean statement to send them.

    Customer statements as PDF, print or WhatsApp, with every payment on the ledger.

  • The problem

    Damaged pieces never counted

    A scratched table or a broken chair is moved to the back, but stock still counts it.

    Record it as damage or return it to the supplier. Stock and cost stay right.
