The problems

Common problems bag shop owners face without POS software

One backpack comes in five colours, a trolley comes in three sizes, and the buyer who took the black one wants the blue one tomorrow. A register cannot keep up with all of this. Good bag shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Days of typing product names

    Backpacks, ladies handbags, laptop bags, travel bags, trolleys and wallets all need a name and a category. Typing them one by one takes days away from selling.

    Pick from 680+ bag products already loaded. Search, tick and add, then set your own stock and price.

  • The problem

    One model, many colours

    The stock book says 30 pieces of one backpack. Nobody knows how many are black, how many are maroon, and which colour finished last week.

    The variant generator makes every model and colour in one go, each with its own stock and price.

  • The problem

    Exchanges that break the stock count

    A buyer brings a handbag back for another colour. The old bill goes in a drawer, a new one gets written, and the stock is now wrong on both colours.

    Handle the exchange on the same bill. It keeps the same invoice number, so the record stays clean.

  • The problem

    A new price from every salesman

    Staff tell the price from memory. One says Rs 3,200, the other says Rs 2,800, and the buyer starts bargaining with both of them.

    Scan a barcode or search the name, and the same fixed price comes on every bill.

  • The problem

    Udhaar on loose slips

    Regular buyers and small shops take bags on credit. The slips go missing, and nobody agrees on the balance.

    Every udhaar sale and payment sits on the customer ledger. Send a statement on WhatsApp in a few taps.

  • The problem

    Season rush and load-shedding

    School reopening, Eid and the Hajj and Umrah months bring crowds together. Then the light goes, the internet goes, and buyers walk to the next shop.

    Our bag shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Bag shop POS software questions

Which is the best bag shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best bag shop POS software is the one built for the way a bag shop sells. Check five things: a ready product list, stock for every model and colour, clean exchanges, barcode billing, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Do I need to type all my bags myself?

No. UpTrendPos has 680+ bag products already loaded. Search, tick the ones you sell and add them to your shop. If a product is missing, add it yourself in seconds. You can also add any product from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products, by name or category.

Does the catalog come with prices and stock?

No. The catalog gives you the product names and details. You set your own stock and price, so your rates are always your own.

Can I keep stock for each model and colour?

Yes. The variant generator takes your models and colours and makes every item in one go. Each item has its own stock and price, so the black and the maroon backpack of the same model never mix.

How do I keep trolley sizes separate?

Use size and colour in the variant generator, like 20, 24 and 28 inch in black, blue and grey. Every size and colour becomes its own item, with its own stock and its own price.

Can a buyer exchange a bag for another colour?

Yes. Handle the exchange on the same bill. Returns and bill edits keep the same invoice number, so you never end up with a second bill for one sale. The buyer can get the updated bill on WhatsApp.

Can I print barcode labels for bags with no tag?

Yes, on Enterprise and Custom plans. Print a label for each model and colour, then scan it at the counter. On every plan you can scan a barcode that is already on the tag, with a USB scanner or your phone camera.

Can regular buyers and small shops buy on udhaar?

Yes. The unpaid part goes on the customer ledger as udhaar. Every later payment sits on the same ledger, and you can send a statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp. Customer installments (qist) with a guarantor are on Enterprise and Custom plans.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does bag shop POS software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits one bag shop, Enterprise suits a busy shop, and Custom suits a small chain. Pay monthly or yearly after a 14-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Add counters with separate staff logins and cash sessions, and each branch keeps its own stock. The owner sees every branch from one account. Limits depend on your plan.
