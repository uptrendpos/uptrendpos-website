FAQ

Sports shop POS software questions

Which is the best sports shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best sports shop POS software is the one built for the way a sports shop sells. Check five things: a ready product list, stock for each size and colour, A4 quotes for schools and clubs, udhaar on a ledger, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 7 days.

Do I need to type all my items myself?

No. UpTrendPos has 250+ sports products already loaded. Search, tick the ones you sell and add them to your shop. If a product is missing, add it yourself in seconds. You can also add any product from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products, by name or category.

Does the catalog come with prices and stock?

No. The catalog gives you the product names and details. You set your own stock and price, so your rates are always your own.

Can I keep stock for each size and colour of a kit or shoe?

Yes. The variant generator takes your sizes and colours and makes every item in one go. Each item has its own stock and price, so a medium blue shirt and a large green shirt never mix.

How do I handle a school or club order?

Make an A4 quotation with your logo and the items, sizes and rates, and send it on WhatsApp. When the school confirms, convert the quote to a sale in one tap. Nothing is typed twice.

Can a school or club pay later?

Yes. Put the sale on udhaar. It goes on that school or club ledger with every payment they make. Send a statement as PDF or on WhatsApp whenever they ask.

Can I sell balls by the piece and by the dozen?

Yes. Set units like piece, pair, set, box and dozen. Sell one ball to a player and a dozen to a club on the same bill, and stock stays right in the selling unit.

Can I print barcode labels for items with no barcode?

Yes, on Enterprise and Custom plans. Print a label for each item and stick it on the tag or the box, then scan it at the counter. On every plan you can scan a barcode that is already on the item, with a USB scanner or your phone camera.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does sports shop POS software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits one sports shop, Enterprise suits a busy store, and Custom suits a small chain. Pay monthly or yearly after a 7-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Can a buyer exchange shoes for another size?

Yes. Make the exchange on the same bill. The invoice number stays the same, the returned pair goes back into stock, and the new size goes out.
