The problems

Common problems watch shop owners face without POS software

A watch is small, but its price is not. One model comes in three dial colours, ten pieces sit in a showcase worth more than the rest of the shop, and every buyer wants to hear what the warranty covers. A register cannot hold all of this. Good watch shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Days of typing product names

    Gents watches, ladies watches, smart watches, straps and watch cells all need a name and a category. Typing them one by one takes days away from selling.

    Pick from 150+ watch products already loaded. Search, tick and add, then set your own stock and price.

  • The problem

    One model, many dial colours

    The stock book says 12 pieces of one model. Nobody knows how many are black, how many are blue, and which colour has finished.

    The variant generator makes every model and colour in one go, each with its own stock and price.

  • The problem

    Warranty promises on memory

    The salesman says one year on the machine. Months later the buyer comes back and each side remembers it differently.

    Write the warranty note on the sale, so what you promised stays on record with that bill.

  • The problem

    A new price from every salesman

    Staff tell the price from memory. One says Rs 8,500, the other says Rs 7,800, and the buyer starts bargaining with both.

    Scan the barcode or search the name, and the same fixed price comes on every bill.

  • The problem

    Small pieces, big losses

    One watch missing from the showcase can eat the profit of many sales. Without a count, you find out weeks later, or never.

    Run a stock audit, record damage, and give each staff member an own login and cash session.

  • The problem

    Eid rush and load-shedding

    Eid and the wedding season bring gift buyers all at once. The light goes, the internet goes, and buyers walk to the next shop.

    Our watch shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Watch shop POS software questions

Which is the best watch shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best watch shop POS software is the one built for the way a watch shop sells. Check five things: a ready product list, stock for every model and colour, warranty notes on the sale, barcode billing, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Do I need to type all my watches myself?

No. UpTrendPos has 150+ watch products already loaded. Search, tick the ones you sell and add them to your shop. If a product is missing, add it yourself in seconds. You can also add any product from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products, by name or category.

Does the catalog come with prices and stock?

No. The catalog gives you the product names and details. You set your own stock and price, so your rates are always your own.

Can I keep stock for each model and dial colour?

Yes. The variant generator takes your models and colours and makes every item in one go. Each item has its own stock and price, so the black and the blue dial of the same model never mix.

Can I write the warranty on the sale?

Yes. Add a warranty note to the sale, like one year on the machine or six months on the battery. It stays with that sale, and the buyer gets the bill as a paper print or on WhatsApp.

Can I manage watch repairs?

Repair job cards are coming soon. Today you can sell straps, watch cells and other small items on a normal bill, so that money and stock stay on record.

Can I scan barcodes on watch boxes?

Yes. Use a USB barcode scanner or your phone camera. The item and its fixed price come on the bill. Barcode label printing for pieces with no barcode is on Enterprise and Custom plans.

Can regular buyers and shops buy on udhaar?

Yes. The unpaid part goes on the customer ledger as udhaar. Every later payment sits on the same ledger, and you can send a statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp. Customer installments (qist) with a guarantor are on Enterprise and Custom plans.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does watch shop POS software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits one watch shop, Enterprise suits a busy shop, and Custom suits a small chain. Pay monthly or yearly after a 14-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Add counters with separate staff logins and cash sessions, and each branch keeps its own stock. The owner sees every branch from one account. Limits depend on your plan.
