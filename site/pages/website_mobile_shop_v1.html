Does it save the IMEI of every phone?

Yes. Save the IMEI or serial number when the phone comes in on the GRN, and the same IMEI is picked when you sell it. The phone can be traced from the supplier to the customer at any time.

How does warranty lookup work?

Type or scan the IMEI or serial number of a sold phone. You see when it was sold, the bill number, the customer and the warranty. This helps you handle claims with proof.

Can I sell phones on qist?

Yes, on the Enterprise and Custom plans. Set the advance, the markup and the number of months. The plan shows every monthly due, and markup income is kept apart until it is earned.

Can this mobile shop software also run a computer shop?

Yes. Laptops, tablets and parts work the same way, with serial numbers instead of IMEIs. Mouse, keyboards, cables and other accessories sell like normal items with quick bill.

Can I sell accessories fast?

Yes. Scan the barcode or search the item, or use quick bill and the calculator. Covers, chargers and cables go on the same bill as the phone, and the bill can go on WhatsApp.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar and qist need internet so every device shows the same balance.
