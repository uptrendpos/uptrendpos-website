The problems

Common problems showroom owners face without software

A showroom sells costly units, often on qist, and every unit has its own chassis number and warranty. One mistake in a register can cost a full month's profit. Good vehicle showroom software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Which chassis did we sell him?

    A customer comes back for warranty or papers, and nobody can find which unit, which chassis or which bill it was.

    The chassis number of every unit is saved from the supplier's delivery to the sale.

  • The problem

    Qist kept in a diary

    Down payments, monthly dues and guarantors are written by hand, and late payers are found too late.

    Qist plans with advance, markup, monthly dues and guarantor details for every customer. Enterprise+

  • The problem

    Warranty claims without proof

    Customers ask for warranty on a unit you are not sure you sold, or sold long ago.

    Warranty lookup by chassis number shows the sale, the bill and the warranty date.

  • The problem

    Models and colours mixed up

    Same model, two years and four colours. Stock in the register never matches the floor.

    The variant generator makes every model year and colour as its own item with its own units.

  • The problem

    Quotes lost on paper

    A buyer asks for a price with accessories, goes home to think, and the paper quote is gone.

    A4 quotation on WhatsApp that turns into a sale in one tap when the buyer comes back.

  • The problem

    Cash short after a big sale day

    Large cash amounts, many staff and one drawer. At night the cash does not match.

    Staff logins and cash sessions per shift show who sold what and who took cash.

FAQ

Vehicle showroom software questions

Which is the best vehicle showroom software in Pakistan?

The best vehicle showroom software is the one built for units, not groceries. Check five things: chassis number tracking, warranty lookup, qist with a guarantor, A4 quotes and invoices, and offline billing. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Is this motorcycle showroom software too?

Yes. Turn on the Motorcycles group and add each model with its engine cc, start type, wheels and brakes. Model years and colours become separate items, each with its own units and chassis numbers.

Can I sell electric bikes and scooters?

Yes. The Electric Bikes and Scooters group has fields for battery type, battery capacity, range per charge, motor power, charging time and battery warranty.

Does it work for a car showroom?

Yes. The Cars and SUVs group has fields for trim, transmission, fuel type, engine cc, seats and local or imported. You can sell new, used and reconditioned cars.

Does it save the chassis number of every unit?

Yes. Save the chassis number when the unit comes in on the GRN, and the same number is picked when you sell it. Today one number is saved per unit. A second number, such as the engine or battery serial, is coming soon.

Can I sell on qist with a guarantor?

Yes, on the Enterprise and Custom plans. Set the advance, the markup and the number of months, and add the guarantor's name, CNIC and phone. The plan shows every monthly due, and markup income is kept apart until it is earned.

Can I give a buyer a quotation first?

Yes. Make an A4 quotation with the unit and accessories and send it on WhatsApp. When the buyer comes back, convert it to a sale in one tap.

Does it track registration and documents?

Not yet. Registration status and document hand-over tracking are coming soon. Today you can run units, chassis numbers, qist, warranty, purchases and full accounts in one place.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar and qist need internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does vehicle showroom software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits a small dealer, Enterprise suits a showroom that sells on qist, and Custom suits a dealer with branches. Pay monthly or yearly after a 14-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Do you have a ready list of bike and car models?
