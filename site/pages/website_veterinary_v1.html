The problems

Common problems vet stores face without POS software

A veterinary store keeps hundreds of small items with a date on them. One dewormer comes in a box of strips, one tonic in bottles of three sizes, and dairy farmers buy on udhaar and pay when the milk money comes. A register and a calculator cannot keep up. Good veterinary store POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Medicine expires on the shelf

    A box of animal medicine sits behind newer stock. When you find it, the date has passed and the money is gone.

    Batch number and expiry date on every purchase. Expiring and expired alerts tell you what to sell first or return.

  • The problem

    Strips and pieces mixed up

    You buy a box of 10 strips, but one farmer wants 2 strips and another wants 3 pieces. The stock count turns into a guess.

    Units: sell by the box, strip or piece, bottles and sachets too. Stock is always kept in the unit you sell.

  • The problem

    Farmer udhaar in a diary

    Dairy farmers take medicine and supplements every week and pay at the end of the month. The diary has cuttings and the total never matches.

    Every sale on the customer ledger with every payment. Send a clear statement on WhatsApp when payment is due.

  • The problem

    Same item, many sizes

    A tonic comes in 100 ml, 500 ml and 1 litre. A powder comes in 250 g and 1 kg. Each size has its own price and its own stock.

    The variant generator makes every size of one item in one go, each with its own stock and price.

  • The problem

    Supplier balance unclear

    You take stock from many distributors. Nobody knows what is still due to each one and which bill is old.

    Purchase invoice, goods received (GRN) and a supplier ledger with the balance due and how old each bill is.

  • The problem

    Load-shedding stops the counter

    The light goes, the internet goes, and a farmer is waiting at the counter with cash in hand.

    Our veterinary store POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Veterinary store POS software questions

Which is the best veterinary store POS software in Pakistan?

The best veterinary store POS software is the one built for the way a vet store works. Check five things: box, strip and piece units, batch and expiry alerts for animal medicines, udhaar for farmers, supplier ledgers, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 7 days.

Can I sell a medicine by the box, strip and piece?

Yes. Set the units for each item once. Sell a full box, a strip or a single piece, and stock always stays in the selling unit. Tonics can sell by the bottle or carton and supplements by the bag or kg, all on one bill.

How do expiry alerts work for animal medicines?

Enter the batch number and expiry date when stock comes in. UpTrendPos shows expiring and expired batches, and the batch-wise stock and expiry report shows what to sell first or return to the supplier.

Can dairy and poultry farmers buy on udhaar?

Yes. Every unpaid sale goes on the farmer's customer ledger, and every payment sits on the same ledger. Send the statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp and collect the balance when it is due.

Can a farmer pay in parts?

Yes. Receive any amount at any time on the customer ledger, and the balance updates on its own. On Enterprise and Custom plans you can also set qist plans with a guarantor.

Do you have a ready list of veterinary items?

A starter list of 50+ veterinary items is ready to add; you add the rest of your stock yourself. The variant generator makes, for example, every bottle size of one tonic in one go. You can also add products from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products by name or category.

Can I return expired or damaged stock to the supplier?

Yes. Make a return to the supplier, and stock and the supplier balance update on their own. Stock that cannot go back is recorded as damage, so your stock stays true.

Does it give advice on which medicine or dose to give an animal?

No. UpTrendPos is software for billing, stock, udhaar and accounts. It does not give treatment or dose advice. That stays with you and the vet.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar and qist need internet so every device shows the same balance.
