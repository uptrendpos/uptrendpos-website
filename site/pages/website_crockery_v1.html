The problems

Common problems crockery shop owners face without POS software

Glasses come in a carton of 48, a buyer wants a set of 6, and another wants just one cup to replace a broken one. A register cannot keep up with all of this. Good crockery shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Days of typing product names

    Dinner sets, tea sets, glasses, mugs, jugs, pots, plastic ware and kitchen tools all need a name and a category. Typing them one by one takes days away from selling.

    Pick from 2,000+ crockery and household products already loaded. Search, tick and add, then set your own stock and price.

  • The problem

    Cartons, sets and loose pieces

    You buy glasses by the carton, sell them in sets of 6, and sometimes sell one piece. The stock book never matches what is on the shelf.

    Units on one bill: carton, set or piece. Stock always stays in the selling unit, so every sale drops the right count.

  • The problem

    Broken pieces nobody counts

    A cup cracks in the carton, a plate falls off the shelf, a glass breaks while packing. Nobody writes it down, and the stock count goes wrong.

    Record every broken piece as damage in a few taps. Stock and the loss both stay on record.

  • The problem

    A new price from every salesman

    Staff tell the price from memory. One says Rs 4,500 for the set, the other says Rs 3,900, and the buyer starts bargaining with both of them.

    Scan a barcode or search the name, and the same fixed price comes on every bill.

  • The problem

    Udhaar on loose slips

    Regular families, hotels and caterers take crockery on credit. The slips go missing, and nobody agrees on the balance.

    Every udhaar sale and payment sits on the customer ledger. Send a statement on WhatsApp in a few taps.

  • The problem

    Wedding season and load-shedding

    The wedding season and Eid bring dowry and gift buyers together. Then the light goes, the internet goes, and buyers walk to the next shop.

    Our crockery shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Crockery shop POS software questions

Which is the best crockery shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best crockery shop POS software is the one built for the way a crockery shop sells. Check five things: a ready product list, carton, set and piece units, breakage records, barcode billing, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Do I need to type all my products myself?

No. UpTrendPos has 2,000+ crockery and household products already loaded. Search, tick the ones you sell and add them to your shop. If a product is missing, add it yourself in seconds. You can also add any product from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products, by name or category.

Does the catalog come with prices and stock?

No. The catalog gives you the product names and details. You set your own stock and price, so your rates are always your own.

Can I sell a carton, a set and a single piece of the same item?

Yes. Set the units once, like a carton of 48, a set of 6 and a piece. Pick the unit on the bill. Stock always stays in the selling unit, so every sale drops the right count.

How do I record broken crockery?

Open inventory movements and record the broken pieces as damage. Stock goes down and the loss stays on record. If a carton came broken from the supplier, make a return to the supplier instead.

Can I keep stock for each design and colour?

Yes. The variant generator takes your designs and colours and makes every item in one go. Each item has its own stock and price, so two patterns of the same mug never mix.

Can I print barcode labels for sets with no barcode?

Yes, on Enterprise and Custom plans. Print a label for each item and unit, then scan it at the counter. On every plan you can scan a barcode that is already on the box, with a USB scanner or your phone camera.

Can hotels, caterers and families buy on udhaar?

Yes. The unpaid part goes on the customer ledger as udhaar. Every later payment sits on the same ledger, and you can send a statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp. Customer installments (qist) with a guarantor are on Enterprise and Custom plans.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does crockery shop POS software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits one crockery shop, Enterprise suits a busy store, and Custom suits a small chain. Pay monthly or yearly after a 14-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Add counters with separate staff logins and cash sessions, and each branch keeps its own stock. The owner sees every branch from one account. Limits depend on your plan.
