Quick fixes

Fix common problems in a minute

Try these steps first. If the problem stays, send us a WhatsApp message with a screenshot.

Sales are not syncing

  1. Check the internet on that device
  2. Open the app and wait on the home screen for a minute
  3. Look for the sync banner and its message
  4. Still stuck? Do not uninstall. Send us a screenshot.

Printer is not printing

  1. Check paper, power and the cable or Bluetooth
  2. On a phone, plug the Type-C cable in again
  3. Select the printer again in print settings
  4. Print a test receipt, then send us the model

Cannot log in

  1. Owners: use Forgot Password on the login screen
  2. Staff: ask the owner or admin to reset your PIN
  3. Check the mobile number you are typing
  4. Login needs internet the first time

App says offline too long

  1. Connect the device to the internet
  2. Open the app and let it check your plan
  3. Do not change the phone date or time
  4. Your data on the device is safe

FBR invoice not reported

  1. FBR reporting needs internet
  2. Check NTN, STRN, POS ID and token in FBR settings
  3. Test in sandbox before production
  4. Send us the invoice number and error

Scanner is not reading

  1. Click inside the search box first
  2. Try a different barcode to test
  3. Re-plug the USB scanner
  4. Check the barcode is saved on the item

FAQ

Support questions

What are your support hours?

Our support hours are Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm, Pakistan time. Messages sent on Sunday, public holidays or after hours are answered on the next working day. When billing has fully stopped, we handle it first.

Is support free?

Yes. Support is included in every paid plan at no extra cost. That covers how-to questions, sync and printer help, FBR setup help and fixing problems in the app. Custom work, data entry for you and on-site visits are quoted separately if you need them.

Can you help me set up my shop?

Yes. After you sign up, our team helps you add your items, connect your printer and scanner, set up staff logins and branches, and train your staff. Send your item list in Excel or a clear photo and we help you load it.

How do I export my data or delete my account?

Export lists and reports as PDF or CSV from the app. To delete your account, the shop admin can use the app menu, or email us from your registered contact with your shop name. Export what you need first, because deleted data cannot be brought back.

Do you give support in Urdu?

Yes. Our team supports you in Urdu and English on WhatsApp, phone and email. You can send voice notes, screenshots or short videos on WhatsApp, which is often the fastest way to show us the problem.

Can you see my shop data when I ask for help?

Only when you ask for help and only what we need to fix the problem. Support access to a shop is limited and logged. We never ask for your password or login code. If someone asks for them, it is not us.

Need help right now?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your shop name and a screenshot. We will take it from there.
