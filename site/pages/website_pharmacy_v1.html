The problems

Common problems medical store owners face without POS software

A medical store sells thousands of items with batches, expiry dates and strict rules. Paper registers and simple billing apps cannot keep up. Good pharmacy POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Typing thousands of medicines

    A new medical store can spend weeks entering medicine names by hand. Most pharmacy software in Pakistan starts you with an empty list.

    Pick from 17,000+ medicines already loaded. Search, tick and add.

  • The problem

    Wrong strength given

    Two strengths of one medicine look the same in a quick search.

    Strength and form show on every search result, so the right one is picked.

  • The problem

    Expiry found too late

    Syrups, drops and injections expire on the shelf before anyone checks.

    Batch-wise expiry alerts show what expires in the next 30 days.

  • The problem

    Box, strip or tablet mix-up

    Selling loose tablets from a strip breaks the stock count.

    Set packing once, like 1 box = 10 strips. Sell any unit, stock stays right.

  • The problem

    Controlled medicine without a check

    A busy counter can sell a controlled medicine without looking at the prescription.

    Rx items show a red badge. Controlled items need a "prescription checked" tick.

  • The problem

    Medicine not found on the shelf

    Helpers waste time looking through racks while the customer waits.

    Every item keeps its rack number, shown right at billing.

FAQ

Pharmacy POS software questions

Which is the best pharmacy POS software in Pakistan?

The best pharmacy POS software is the one built for a medical store counter. Check five things: medicines already loaded, salt search, batch and expiry alerts, box-strip-tablet selling and offline billing. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Do I need to type all medicines myself?

No. UpTrendPos has a global catalog of 17,000+ medicines in every plan. Search, tick the ones you sell and add them to your pharmacy. If a product is missing, add it yourself in a minute. You then set your own prices and stock. A pharmacy can also add baby, grocery and general-store items from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products.

Does the medicine catalog include prices?

No. The catalog gives you medicine names, form, strength and salt. You set your own cost, sale price, stock, packing, rack and expiry when stock comes in through a GRN. On your next order, your saved rack and alert levels come back as they were.

Can I search medicines by salt or generic name?

Yes. Type the salt and you see every brand you have with that salt, with strength, form and stock. You can also search by brand, batch or barcode. This helps when a customer asks for a cheaper brand of the same medicine.

How does this medical store software track expiry?

Enter the batch and expiry date when stock comes in. The dashboard shows expiring and expired items, and the batch-wise report lists every batch with its date. The oldest batch sells first, and you can return expired stock to the distributor.

Can I sell by box, strip and single tablet?

Yes. Set the packing when you buy, for example 1 box = 10 strips and 1 strip = 10 tablets. At the counter, sell a box, a strip or loose tablets. The bill and your stock both use the right unit, so the stock count stays correct.

How are prescription and controlled medicines handled?

Prescription medicines show a red Rx badge on search and on the bill. Controlled medicines need the cashier to tick "prescription checked" before the bill can be saved. This keeps the counter fast while making sure the check is never skipped.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Our pharmacy POS software keeps cash billing running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later, with no double entries. Udhaar sales need internet so every device shows the same khata.

Does UpTrendPos support FBR invoices for pharmacies?

Yes, on the Enterprise and Custom plans. If your pharmacy is a Tier-1 retailer, add your FBR details once. Each bill then prints an FBR invoice number, QR code and the Re 1 POS fee. Pharmacies not on FBR POS can keep it off.

How much does pharmacy POS software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits one medical store, Enterprise suits a busy pharmacy and Custom suits a small chain. Every plan includes the medicine catalog. Pay monthly or yearly after a 14-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. UpTrendPos pharmacy POS software lets you add counters with separate staff logins, and each branch keeps its own stock, batches and prices. The owner sees every branch from one account. Branch and user limits depend on your plan, and we can quote for more.
