The problems

Common problems jewellery shop owners face without POS software

A jewellery shop sells small pieces of high value. The gold rate moves, making charges differ, weights must be exact and many customers pay in parts. A calculator and a receipt book cannot keep all of this straight. Good jewellery shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Bills worked out on a calculator

    Every morning the rate changes. Your salesman works out each bill on a calculator and writes it by hand, and one wrong figure costs you money.

    Daily gold rate and making charge on the bill. The bill is printed or sent on WhatsApp, clean and the same every time.

  • The problem

    Weights in a rough register

    Pieces are weighed and the weight is written in a register. Later nobody can read it, and the stock in the safe does not match the book.

    Weights in grams on the item and on the bill, so the record and the safe tell the same story.

  • The problem

    Which design is in stock?

    The same ring comes in three designs and many sizes. A customer asks for size 14 and you open every box to check.

    Every design in every size is its own item with its own stock. You see it on the screen in a second.

  • The problem

    Udhaar in a khata

    Families pay part now and part after the wedding. The khata gets full of cuttings and you lose track of who owes what.

    Udhaar on the customer ledger with every payment, and a statement on WhatsApp in one tap.

  • The problem

    Qist in a register

    A bridal set is sold on monthly qist. The register never matches what was collected, and the guarantor's number is on a loose slip.

    Qist plans with a guarantor on Enterprise and Custom, with each installment on the record.

  • The problem

    Costly pieces, weak control

    Many hands touch the counter. A bill is changed or deleted and you never find out who did it.

    Own login for each staff member, cash sessions per shift, and an audit trail of deletes and changes on Enterprise and Custom.
