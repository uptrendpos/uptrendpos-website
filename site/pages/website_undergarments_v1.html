The problems

Common problems undergarments shop owners face without POS software

One vest comes in five sizes and three colours, socks come by the dozen but go out by the pair, and the buyer who took medium wants large tomorrow. A register cannot keep up with all of this. Good undergarments shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Days of typing product names

    Vests, banyans, briefs, boxers, socks, stockings, thermal wear and nightwear all need a name, a size and a category. Typing them one by one takes days.

    Pick from 400+ products already loaded. Search, tick and add, then set your own stock and price.

  • The problem

    One item, many sizes

    The stock book says 60 pieces of one vest. Nobody knows how many are medium, how many are XL, and which size finished last week.

    The variant generator makes every size and colour in one go, each with its own stock and price.

  • The problem

    Dozen, pack or single piece

    Socks come in by the dozen. One buyer takes a pair, the next takes a pack of 3, a small shop takes two dozen. The count never matches.

    Units sell the piece, the pack of 3 and the dozen from one stock, kept in pieces.

  • The problem

    Size exchanges that break the count

    A buyer brings a vest back for a bigger size. A new bill gets written and the stock goes wrong on both sizes.

    Handle the exchange on the same bill. It keeps the same invoice number, so the record stays clean.

  • The problem

    Udhaar on loose slips

    Mohalla shops take packs on credit. The slips go missing, and nobody agrees on the balance.

    Every udhaar sale and payment sits on the customer ledger. Send a statement on WhatsApp in a few taps.

  • The problem

    Winter and Eid rush with load-shedding

    The first cold week and Eid bring crowds. Then the light and the internet go, and buyers walk to the next shop.

    Our undergarments shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Undergarments shop POS software questions

Which is the best undergarments shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best undergarments shop POS software is the one built for the way a hosiery shop sells. Check five things: a ready product list, stock for every size and colour, pack and dozen billing, clean exchanges, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Do I need to type all my products myself?

No. UpTrendPos has 400+ undergarments and hosiery products already loaded. Search, tick the ones you sell and add them to your shop. If a product is missing, add it yourself in seconds. You can also add any product from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products, by name or category.

Does the catalog come with prices and stock?

No. The catalog gives you the product names and details. You set your own stock and price, so your rates are always your own.

Can I keep stock for each size and colour?

Yes. The variant generator takes your sizes and colours and makes every item in one go. Each item has its own stock and price, so medium white and large black of the same vest never mix.

Can I sell socks by the pair, the pack of 3 and the dozen?

Yes. Set the units once. Buy by the dozen, then sell a single pair, a pack of 3 or a full dozen on the same bill. Stock stays in pieces, so every sale takes the right count from one stock.

Can a buyer exchange an item for another size?

Yes. Handle the exchange on the same bill. Returns and bill edits keep the same invoice number, so you never end up with a second bill for one sale. The buyer can get the updated bill on WhatsApp.

Can I print barcode labels for loose stock?

Yes, on Enterprise and Custom plans. Print a label for each size and colour, then scan it at the counter. On every plan you can scan a barcode that is already on the pack, with a USB scanner or your phone camera.

Can regular buyers and small shops buy on udhaar?

Yes. The unpaid part goes on the customer ledger as udhaar. Send a statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp. Customer installments (qist) with a guarantor are on Enterprise and Custom plans.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does undergarments shop POS software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits one shop, Enterprise suits a busy shop, and Custom suits a small chain. Pay monthly or yearly after a 14-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Add counters with separate staff logins and cash sessions, and each branch keeps its own stock. Limits depend on your plan.
