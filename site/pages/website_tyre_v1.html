The problems

Common problems tyre shop owners face without POS software

A tyre shop keeps the same tyre in many sizes and rims. Transporters take a full set on udhaar, car owners ask about warranty, and stock is stacked high in the godown. A register and a calculator cannot keep all of this straight. Good tyre shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Which size is in stock?

    A customer asks for 185/65 R15. Your boy climbs the stack in the godown, reads every sidewall, and comes back to say "yeh size khatam hai".

    Every tyre in every size and rim is its own item with its own stock. You see it on the screen in a second.

  • The problem

    Warranty on a loose slip

    You write the warranty on a slip. The customer loses it, then comes back months later and argues about the date and the shop.

    Warranty notes on the sale, printed on the bill or sent on WhatsApp as a picture or PDF, so the promise is on record.

  • The problem

    Transporters' udhaar in a khata

    Truck owners and workshops take tyres on udhaar every month. The khata fills with cuttings and nobody agrees on the balance.

    Udhaar on the customer ledger with every payment, and a statement on WhatsApp in one tap.

  • The problem

    Big orders paid in parts

    A transporter needs six truck tyres at once and wants to pay monthly. The register never matches what was collected.

    Qist plans with a guarantor on Enterprise and Custom, with each installment on the record.

  • The problem

    Faulty tyre from the supplier

    A new tyre comes in with a fault. It sits in a corner, the supplier never gives credit, and your stock count is wrong.

    Record it as damage or return it to the supplier, so stock and the supplier balance stay right.

  • The problem

    Load-shedding stops the counter

    The light goes, the internet goes, and a driver with a flat tyre is waiting at your counter.

    Our tyre shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

Can I keep stock for each tyre size and rim?

Yes. Use the variant generator: pick the tyre, its sizes (width and ratio) and its rim sizes, and every item is made in one go with its own code, stock and price.

Can I write the warranty on the bill?

Yes. Write the warranty note on the sale in your own words. It shows on the thermal receipt or A4 invoice, and on the bill you send on WhatsApp as a picture or PDF.

Can I check warranty by the tyre serial number?

Not for tyre shops today. Warranty notes are written on the sale, and you find the bill on the customer's ledger by name or phone number.

Can transporters and mechanics buy on udhaar?

Yes. The unpaid part goes on the customer ledger as udhaar. Every later payment sits on the same ledger, and you can send a statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp.

Can I sell a set of truck tyres on qist?

Yes, on Enterprise and Custom plans. Set up a qist plan with a guarantor and record each installment. Qist needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

Can I take the old tyre back and give a discount?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar and qist need internet so every device shows the same balance.
