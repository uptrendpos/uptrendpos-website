Common problems stationery shop owners face without POS software

A stationery shop sells hundreds of small items: one pencil to a child, a full box of pens to an office, and 300 notebooks to a school on udhaar. Good stationery shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Hundreds of small items to type

    Pens, pencils, erasers, sharpeners, notebooks, registers, files, glue, colours and paper all need a name and a category. Typing them one by one takes days.

    Pick from 200+ stationery products already loaded. Search, tick and add, then set your own stock and price.

  • The problem

    A box sold as loose pieces

    You buy pens by the box and sell them one by one. After a month nobody knows how many boxes and how many loose pens are left.

    Set units once: carton, box, dozen and piece. Sell one pen or a full box on the same bill, and stock stays right in the selling unit.

    School season rush

    When the new session starts, parents with book lists fill the shop. The salesman checks every rate by hand and the line keeps growing.

    Scan a barcode or search the name. The price comes on its own and the bill is ready in seconds.

  • The problem

    School orders on a paper slip

    A school asks for notebooks and registers for the new session. You write rates on a slip, and a week later nobody remembers the price you gave.

    Make an A4 quotation with your logo, send it on WhatsApp, and convert it to a sale in one tap when the order is confirmed.

  • The problem

    Office udhaar in a diary

    Offices take paper, files and pens every week and pay at the end of the month. The diary says one thing, the office clerk says another.

    Every sale and payment goes on that office or school ledger. Send a clean statement on WhatsApp in one tap.

FAQ

Stationery shop POS software questions

Which is the best stationery shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best stationery shop POS software is the one built for the way a stationery shop sells. Check five things: a ready product list, box and piece units, A4 quotes for schools, udhaar on a ledger, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 7 days.

Do I need to type all my items myself?

No. UpTrendPos has 200+ stationery products already loaded. Search, tick the ones you sell and add them to your shop. If a product is missing, add it yourself in seconds. You can also add any product from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products, by name or category.

Does the catalog come with prices and stock?

No. The catalog gives you the product names and details. You set your own stock and price, so your rates are always your own.

Can I sell pens by the box and by the piece?

Yes. Set units like carton, box, dozen, ream and piece. Sell one pen to a student and a full box to an office on the same bill, and stock stays right in the selling unit.

How do I handle a school order for the new session?

Make an A4 quotation with your logo and the items, quantities and rates, and send it on WhatsApp. When the school confirms, convert the quote to a sale in one tap. Nothing is typed twice.

Can a school or office pay later?

Yes. Put the sale on udhaar. It goes on that school or office ledger with every payment they make. Send a statement as PDF or on WhatsApp whenever they ask.

Can I sell books as well as stationery?

Yes. Add books as products and sort them by category, like class, subject or story books. Bill them with a barcode or by name, next to pens and notebooks on the same bill.

Can I print barcode labels for items with no barcode?

Yes, on Enterprise and Custom plans. Print a label for each item and stick it on the item or the shelf, then scan it at the counter. On every plan you can scan a barcode that is already on the item, with a USB scanner or your phone camera.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does stationery shop POS software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits one stationery shop, Enterprise suits a busy store, and Custom suits a small chain. Pay monthly or yearly after a 7-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Can a parent return or exchange an item?

Yes. Make the return or exchange on the same bill. The invoice number stays the same, the returned item goes back into stock, and the new item goes out.
