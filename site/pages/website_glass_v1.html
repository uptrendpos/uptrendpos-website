The problems

Common problems glass shop owners face without POS software

A glass shop works on quotes. A customer wants a rate for six windows, pays an advance, and the final bill comes a week later. Contractors take material on udhaar, and a sheet can crack on the way in. A diary and a calculator lose track of all this. Good glass shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Quotes on a rough slip

    You write a rate for a shower screen on paper. Two days later the customer comes back and nobody remembers the rate or the size you gave.

    An A4 quotation with your logo, sent on WhatsApp and saved. Turn it into a sale in one tap when the customer says yes.

  • The problem

    Advance money forgotten

    A customer paid Rs 20,000 advance for the windows. When the final bill is made, nobody can find the slip and the customer argues.

    The advance sits on the customer ledger, so the final bill shows exactly what is still due.

  • The problem

    Square feet in the head

    Glass is bought in big sheets and sold by the square foot. Your stock count is a guess, and you only know a sheet is finished when the rack is empty.

    Units: sell glass by the sq ft, aluminium by the foot and fittings by the piece. Stock drops in the unit you sell.

  • The problem

    Contractor udhaar in a diary

    Builders and window makers take material every week. The diary fills with cuttings and nobody agrees on the balance.

    Udhaar on the customer ledger with every payment, and a statement on WhatsApp in one tap.

  • The problem

    Broken glass, wrong stock

    A sheet cracks while unloading or cutting. It goes in the scrap pile, but the stock still shows it as there.

    Record breakage as damage, or return a faulty sheet to the supplier, so stock and the supplier balance stay right.

  • The problem

    Load-shedding stops the counter

    The light goes, the internet goes, and a customer with a site order is waiting at your counter.

    Our glass shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Glass shop POS software questions

Which is the best glass shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best glass shop POS software is the one built for the way a glass shop works. Check five things: A4 quotations that turn into a sale, square feet and foot units, advance on the customer ledger, udhaar for contractors, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Can I send a quotation before the final bill?

Yes. Make an A4 quotation with your logo and send it on WhatsApp. When the customer agrees, convert it to a sale in one tap, so you do not type the items again.

Can I sell glass by the square foot?

Yes. Set the unit to square feet for glass, feet for aluminium sections and pieces for fittings. All three can be on one bill, and stock drops in the unit you sell.

Does it work out square feet from width and height?

No. You enter the square feet on the bill yourself. UpTrendPos does not calculate sizes or plan cutting from a sheet.

How do I record an advance payment?

Record the advance on the customer ledger when the customer pays. When you make the final bill, the ledger shows the advance and the balance still due. Send the statement on WhatsApp.

Can contractors and builders buy on udhaar?

Yes. The unpaid part goes on the customer ledger as udhaar. Every later payment sits on the same ledger, and you can send a statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp.

What happens when a sheet breaks?

Record it as a damage entry, so stock stays right. If the sheet came faulty from the supplier, return it to the supplier and the supplier balance updates.

Do you have a ready list of glass and aluminium items?

No. Your shop starts with an empty list and you add only what you sell. The variant generator makes, for example, every thickness in every shade in one go. You can also add products from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products by name or category.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar and qist need internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does glass shop POS software cost?

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Add counters with separate staff logins and cash sessions, and each branch keeps its own stock. The owner sees every branch from one account. Limits depend on your plan.
