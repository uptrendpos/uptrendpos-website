The problems

Common problems auto parts shop owners face without POS software

An auto spare parts shop has thousands of small parts with codes, many suppliers and a lot of credit to mechanics. A register and memory cannot keep up. Good auto parts POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Mechanic waits while you search

    A mechanic reads out a part code on the phone. You check shelves, boxes and old bills to find the price.

    Type the name or code, or scan the barcode, and see the part with its price and stock in seconds.

  • The problem

    Fast parts run out

    Spark plugs, filters and clutch plates sell every day. You notice they are finished only when a customer asks.

    Low stock alerts and a demand list show what to reorder before it runs out.

  • The problem

    Supplier bills in a file

    You buy from many suppliers on credit. Nobody knows which bill is paid and how much is still due.

    Purchase order, GRN and purchase invoice with a ledger and balance for every supplier.

  • The problem

    Mechanics' udhaar in a diary

    Regular mechanics take parts on credit all month. At the end, nobody agrees on the balance.

    Udhaar khata for every mechanic and workshop, with a statement on WhatsApp.

  • The problem

    Boxes and drums sold loose

    Plugs come in a box of 10, oil comes in a drum, but they sell one piece or one litre at a time.

    Set the box or drum size once. Sell by the piece or litre and stock stays right.

  • The problem

    Returns and wrong parts

    A part comes back because it was the wrong one, or a faulty part goes back to the supplier. Stock and accounts never match.

    Returns keep the same invoice number, and returns to the supplier update stock and the ledger.

FAQ

Auto parts POS software questions

Which is the best auto parts POS software in Pakistan?

The best auto parts POS software is the one built for thousands of parts with codes, not a short grocery list. Check five things: search by name or code, supplier ledgers, udhaar for mechanics, a demand list and offline billing. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Can I find a part by its code?

Yes. Save each part with its own code. Type the code or a few letters of the name, or scan the barcode, and the part shows with its price and stock.

Can it tell me which part fits which vehicle?

No. UpTrendPos finds parts by name, part code or barcode. You can put the vehicle name in the part name, such as "Clutch Plate 70cc", so a search for "70cc" shows those parts.

Does this auto parts shop software keep udhaar for mechanics?

Yes. Every mechanic or workshop gets a khata. Credit sales and payments go on the ledger, and you can send the statement on WhatsApp.

Can I keep supplier bills and balances?

Yes. Make a purchase order, receive goods with a GRN and save the purchase invoice. Each supplier has a ledger with the balance due, and the average cost updates on its own.

How do I know which parts to reorder?

Low stock alerts show parts running low, and the demand list shows what to reorder. Make a purchase order from it and print it in A4 for your supplier.

Can I sell loose oil by the litre and plugs one by one?

Yes. Set the drum or box size once. Sell by the litre or piece, and the stock always shows the right amount.

Do you have a ready list of auto parts?

No. Your shop starts with an empty list and you add the parts you sell, with their codes. The variant generator makes every grade and pack size of an oil in one go.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Add counters with separate staff logins and cash sessions, and each branch keeps its own stock. The owner sees every branch from one account. Limits depend on your plan.
