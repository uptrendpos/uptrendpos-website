The problems

Common problems meat shop owners face without POS software

A meat shop sells by weight, all day, at a fast pace. Rates change, hotels buy on udhaar, and what is not sold today can spoil by tomorrow. A register, a calculator and a few loose slips cannot keep all of this straight. Good meat shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Wrong amount on the calculator

    A customer takes 1.25 kg of chicken and 750 grams of mince. The boy works it out on a calculator in the rush and gets it wrong.

    Type the weight from your scale. The bill multiplies weight by the rate per kg, so the amount is right every time.

  • The problem

    Rates change every day

    Chicken rate goes up in the morning and down at night. Old rates stay on the slip and the counter charges both.

    Change the rate per kg on the item once, and every new bill uses the new rate.

  • The problem

    Hotels and dhabas on udhaar

    Hotels, caterers and regular families take meat on udhaar every day. The khata fills with cuttings and nobody agrees on the balance.

    Udhaar on the customer ledger with every payment, and a statement on WhatsApp in one tap.

  • The problem

    Cash short at night

    Two people work the counter in the day and one at night. At closing the drawer is short and nobody knows which shift it was.

    Cash sessions: each shift opens and closes its own drawer, so the cash is checked against the bills.

  • The problem

    Spoiled stock never recorded

    Meat that goes bad after load-shedding is thrown away, but the book still shows it in stock.

    Record it as damage. Stock stays true and the loss shows in your accounts.

  • The problem

    Load-shedding stops the counter

    The light goes, the internet goes, and the queue for meat on a Sunday morning keeps growing.

    Our meat shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Meat shop POS software questions

Which is the best meat shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best meat shop POS software is the one built for the way a meat shop sells. Check five things: bills by kg and grams, a rate per kg you can change, udhaar for hotels, cash sessions per shift, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Can I sell by kg and by grams?

Yes. Set each item with a rate per kg. At the counter type 1.25 kg or 750 grams and the bill works out the amount. Stock goes down by the exact weight.

Does it connect to my weighing scale by cable?

No. You weigh on your own scale as you do today and type the weight into the bill. It takes a second and works with any scale.

Can I change the chicken rate every day?

Yes. Change the rate per kg on the item and every new bill uses it. Old bills keep the rate they were made with.

Can hotels and caterers buy on udhaar?

Yes. The unpaid part goes on the customer ledger as udhaar. Every later payment sits on the same ledger, and you can send a statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp.

Can I check the cash of each shift?

Yes. Each shift opens a cash session with the opening cash and closes it with the count. The owner sees sales and cash for every shift and cashier.

How do I record meat that spoiled?

Enter it as damage with the weight. Stock goes down and the loss shows in your accounts, so your stock count stays true.

Do you have a ready list of meat items?

No. Your shop starts with an empty list and you add only what you sell, like whole chicken, boneless, mutton, beef, mince and fish. You can also add products from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products by name or category.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.
