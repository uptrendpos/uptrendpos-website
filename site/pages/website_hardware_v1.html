The problems

Common problems hardware shop owners face without POS software

A hardware and sanitary shop has thousands of small items, many sizes of the same fitting and a lot of credit to plumbers and contractors. A register and memory cannot keep up. Good hardware store POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Customer waits while you search

    A plumber asks for a 3/4 inch elbow. You and your helper look through shelves and old bills to find the price.

    Type a few letters and see every matching item with its price and stock in seconds.

  • The problem

    Bags, feet and boxes in one bill

    Cement goes by the bag, pipe by the foot, screws by the piece and tiles by the box. A simple register cannot hold all these units.

    Set the units once for each item. Sell in any unit and stock stays right.

  • The problem

    Quotes for contractors get lost

    A contractor asks for a full bathroom price. The paper quote is lost by the time he comes back to buy.

    Make an A4 quote with your logo, send it on WhatsApp and turn it into a sale in one tap.

  • The problem

    Udhaar in a diary

    Plumbers, masons and contractors take goods on credit all month. At the end, nobody agrees on the balance.

    Udhaar khata for every plumber and contractor, with a statement on WhatsApp.

  • The problem

    Many sizes of one fitting

    The same elbow comes in 1/2, 3/4 and 1 inch, in PVC and PPR. Stock of each size is a guess.

    The variant generator makes every size and type as its own item with its own stock and price.

  • The problem

    Broken and missing items

    A cracked basin, a broken tile box, a missing pack of screws. Stock still shows them as if they are on the shelf.

    Record damage and run a stock audit. Stock and cost stay right.

Which is the best hardware store POS software in Pakistan?

I have thousands of items. How fast can I find one?

Type a few letters of the name, such as "elbow" or "tap", and every matching item shows with its size, price and stock. Items with a barcode can be scanned with a USB scanner or the phone camera.

Can I sell cement by the bag and pipe by the foot?

Yes. Set the unit once for each item: bag for cement, foot or meter for pipe, kg for steel bars. When you sell, the stock drops in the same unit.

Can I sell screws and fittings from a box one by one?

Yes. Set the box or carton size once. Sell by the piece or the full box, and the stock always shows the right number of pieces.

Can I give contractors a quotation?

Yes. Make an A4 quotation with your logo for a bathroom or a full house job and send it on WhatsApp. When the contractor comes back, convert it to a sale in one tap.

Does this hardware shop software keep udhaar for plumbers and contractors?

Does it work during load-shedding?
