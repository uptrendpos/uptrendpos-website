The problems

Common problems paint shop owners face without POS software

A paint shop keeps the same paint in many shades and many pack sizes, and sells a lot on credit to painters and contractors. A register cannot follow this. Good paint store POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Which shade is left?

    Off White sells every day, Sky Blue sits for months. You find out a shade is finished only when a painter asks for it.

    Each shade and pack size is its own item with its own stock, and low stock alerts tell you in time.

  • The problem

    Quarter, gallon and drum mixed up

    The same paint comes in a quarter, a gallon and a drum, each at its own price. Prices get mixed up at the counter.

    Set every pack size once with its own price. Pick the shade and size and the right price comes on the bill.

  • The problem

    Quotes for a full house on paper

    A contractor asks for the price of a full house paint job. The paper quote is lost by the time he comes back.

    Make an A4 quote with your logo, send it on WhatsApp and turn it into a sale in one tap.

  • The problem

    Painters' udhaar in a diary

    Painters and contractors take paint on credit for every job. At the end of the month, nobody agrees on the balance.

    Udhaar khata for every painter and contractor, with a statement on WhatsApp.

  • The problem

    Hundreds of items to add

    One paint in 20 shades and 3 pack sizes is 60 items. Adding them one by one takes days.

    The variant generator makes all shade and size items in one go, each with its own stock and price.

  • The problem

    Dented and leaking tins

    A dented drum or a leaking tin is put aside, but stock still counts it.

    Record it as damage or return it to the supplier. Stock and cost stay right.
