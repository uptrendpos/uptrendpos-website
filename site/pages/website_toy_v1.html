A toy shop has hundreds of small items, the same car in four colours, and a counter full of parents two days before Eid. Good toy shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Days of typing toy names

    Remote cars, dolls, puzzles, blocks, soft toys, board games and gift items all need a name and a category. Typing them one by one takes days away from selling.

    Pick from 1,000+ toy products already loaded. Search, tick and add, then set your own stock and price.

  • The problem

    A long queue in the Eid rush

    Before Eid and in the birthday season, parents stand in a line while the salesman looks for a price and adds it on a calculator. Some leave without buying.

    Scan a barcode or search the name. The price comes on its own and the bill is ready in seconds.

  • The problem

    Same toy, many colours

    The red car sold out but the blue one is still on the shelf, and you only find out when a buyer asks. Stock of each colour is a guess.

    The variant generator makes every model and colour in one go, each with its own stock and price.

    No barcode on many toys

    Many toys and gift sets come from the wholesale market with no barcode. Staff type the name and pick the wrong item.

  • The problem

    Parents ask for the right age

    A buyer wants a gift for a 5 year old. Staff walk around the shop because the stock is not sorted in any useful way.

    Make age groups your categories, like 0-2, 3-5, 6-8 and 9-12 years. Search one age group and see what is in stock.

  • The problem

    The light and the internet go right in the evening rush, and the counter stops while buyers wait.

    Our toy shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Toy shop POS software questions

Which is the best toy shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best toy shop POS software is the one built for the way a toy shop sells. Check five things: a ready product list, stock for each model and colour, fast barcode billing for the Eid rush, udhaar on a ledger, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Do I need to type all my toys myself?

No. UpTrendPos has 1,000+ toy products already loaded. Search, tick the ones you sell and add them to your shop. If a product is missing, add it yourself in seconds. You can also add any product from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products, by name or category.

Does the catalog come with prices and stock?

No. The catalog gives you the product names and details. You set your own stock and price, so your rates are always your own.

Can I sort toys by age group?

Yes. Make age groups your categories, like 0-2, 3-5, 6-8 and 9-12 years, and put each toy in one. When a parent asks for a gift for a certain age, search that category and see what is in stock.

Can I keep stock for each model and colour?

Yes. The variant generator takes your models and colours and makes every item in one go. Each item has its own stock and price, so the red car and the blue car never mix.

How do I sell a gift set?

Add the gift set as one normal item with its own name, barcode and price. It scans and bills like any other toy, and its stock goes down by one with each sale.

Can I print barcode labels for toys with no barcode?

Yes, on Enterprise and Custom plans. Print a label for each item and stick it on the box, then scan it at the counter. On every plan you can scan a barcode that is already on the box, with a USB scanner or your phone camera.

Will it keep up with the Eid and birthday rush?

Yes. Scan or search, and the price comes on its own. Hold a bill while one buyer picks one more toy and bill the next buyer.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does toy shop POS software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits one toy shop, Enterprise suits a busy store, and Custom suits a small chain. Pay monthly or yearly after a 14-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Can a buyer exchange a toy for another colour?

Yes. Make the exchange on the same bill. The invoice number stays the same, the returned toy goes back into stock, and the new one goes out.
