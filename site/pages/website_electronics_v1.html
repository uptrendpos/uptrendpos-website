Yes, on the Enterprise and Custom plans. Set the advance, the markup and the number of months. The plan shows every monthly due, and markup income is kept apart until it is earned.

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar and qist need internet so every device shows the same balance.
