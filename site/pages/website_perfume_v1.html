The problems

Common problems perfume shop owners face without POS software

A perfume shop looks simple from outside. But one fragrance comes in three bottle sizes, attar sells from a big bottle by the ml, and testers leave the shelf every week. A register and a price list in your head cannot hold all of this. Good perfume shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Days of typing product names

    Perfumes, attars, body sprays and gift boxes all need a name, a size and a category. Typing them one by one eats your days.

    Pick from 300+ perfume and attar products already loaded. Search, tick and add, then set your own stock and price.

  • The problem

    One fragrance, many bottles

    The same oud comes in 30 ml, 50 ml and 100 ml. The stock book shows one line, so nobody knows which bottle is finished.

    The variant generator makes every fragrance and size in one go, each with its own stock and price.

  • The problem

    Loose attar with no count

    Attar comes in a big bottle and sells by the ml. After a week nobody can say how much is left or what it earned.

    Buy by the litre and sell by the ml. Stock stays in ml and goes down with every bill.

  • The problem

    Testers vanish from stock

    A new tester is opened, one bottle goes to a regular buyer as a sample, one breaks in the box. The book still counts all three.

    Record testers as sample and broken bottles as damage. Stock and cost stay true.

  • The problem

    A new price from every salesman

    Staff tell the price from memory. One says Rs 3,200, the other says Rs 2,800, and the buyer asks why.

    Scan the barcode or search the name, and the same fixed price comes on every bill.

  • The problem

    Eid rush and load-shedding

    Chand Raat brings the biggest crowd of the year. The light goes, the internet goes, and buyers leave the queue.

    Our perfume shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

FAQ

Perfume shop POS software questions

Which is the best perfume shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best perfume shop POS software is the one built for the way a perfume shop sells. Check five things: a ready product list, stock for every bottle size, loose attar by the ml, tester records, and offline billing in load-shedding. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Do I need to type all my perfumes myself?

No. UpTrendPos has 300+ perfume and attar products already loaded. Search, tick the ones you sell and add them to your shop. If a product is missing, add it yourself in seconds. You can also add any product from the full UpTrendPos catalog of 38,000+ products, by name or category.

Does the catalog come with prices and stock?

No. The catalog gives you the product names and details. You set your own stock and price, so your rates are always your own.

Can I keep stock for each fragrance and bottle size?

Yes. The variant generator takes your fragrances and bottle sizes and makes every item in one go. Each item has its own stock and price, so 50 ml and 100 ml of the same oud never mix.

Can I sell loose attar by the ml?

Yes. Buy attar by the litre and sell it by the ml. Type the ml at the counter and the bill works out the amount. Stock stays in ml and goes down with every sale.

How do I record testers?

Enter each tester as a sample in inventory movements. A broken bottle goes in as damage. Stock goes down and the cost stays on record, so your count matches the shelf.

Can I scan barcodes on perfume bottles?

Yes. Use a USB barcode scanner or your phone camera. The item and its fixed price come on the bill. Barcode label printing for your own bottles is on Enterprise and Custom plans.

Can regular buyers and shops buy on udhaar?

Yes. The unpaid part goes on the customer ledger as udhaar. Every later payment sits on the same ledger, and you can send a statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp.

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes. Cash billing keeps running on the Windows and Android apps when the internet or light goes. Bills save on the device and sync on their own later. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.

How much does perfume shop POS software cost?

It depends on your plan. Premium suits one perfume shop, Enterprise suits a busy shop, and Custom suits a small chain. Pay monthly or yearly after a 14-day free trial. See all prices on our pricing page.

Can I run more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Add counters with separate staff logins and cash sessions, and each branch keeps its own stock. The owner sees every branch from one account. Limits depend on your plan.
