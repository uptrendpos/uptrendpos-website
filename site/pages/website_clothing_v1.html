The problems

Common problems clothing shop owners face without POS software

A garment shop sells one design in many sizes and colours, and most of the year's sales come in a few busy weeks. A register cannot keep up. Good clothing store POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Which sizes are left?

    Stock is counted by design, not by size. A customer asks for a medium and nobody knows if one is left.

    Every size and colour is its own item with its own stock, so you see the medium in seconds.

  • The problem

    Typing the same design 12 times

    One lawn design in 3 colours and 4 sizes means 12 entries. New stock takes days to enter.

    The variant generator makes all 12 items in one go, each with its own code and stock.

  • The problem

    Price written by hand

    Prices are written on paper tags, and staff can change them at the counter.

    Print barcode tags with the size and price. The counter scans the tag, so the price is always right. Enterprise+

  • The problem

    Exchanges spoil the stock count

    A customer swaps a medium for a large, a new bill is made by hand, and the stock goes wrong.

    Edit the bill or take the item back. The bill number stays the same and the stock of both sizes is fixed.

  • The problem

    Long queues in the Eid rush

    Before Eid and the wedding season, customers wait while the cashier writes every bill.

    Scan and bill in seconds, hold a bill for a customer who is still choosing, and add more counters.

  • The problem

    Cash short after a busy day

    Many salesmen, one drawer. At night the cash does not match and nobody knows why.

    Staff logins and cash sessions per shift show who sold what and who took cash.

FAQ

Clothing store POS software questions

Which is the best clothing store POS software in Pakistan?

The best clothing store POS software is the one built for sizes and colours, not groceries. Check five things: stock per size and colour, quick item setup, barcode tags, easy exchanges and offline billing. UpTrendPos does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Can I keep stock for every size and colour?

Yes. Each size and colour of a design is its own item with its own stock and price. When you sell a medium in teal, only that item goes down, so you always know which sizes are left.

Do I have to type every size of a design myself?

No. Type the article once, pick its colours and sizes, and the variant generator makes every item in one go. A design in 3 colours and 4 sizes becomes 12 items in a minute.

Does it come with a ready product list?

No. Clothing changes every season, so your shop starts with an empty list and you add only your own designs. The variant generator makes this fast.

Can I print barcode tags with the size and price?

Yes, on the Enterprise and Custom plans. Print a tag for each piece with its barcode, size, colour and price. On every plan you can scan barcodes that are already on branded stock.

How do exchanges work in this garments shop software?

Open the bill and swap the item for another size or colour, or take it back. The old piece goes back to stock, the new one goes out, and the bill number stays the same, so your records stay clean.

Can it handle the Eid rush?

Yes. Scan tags to bill in seconds, hold a bill while a customer keeps choosing, and run more than one counter with its own staff login. Billing keeps running even if the internet goes off.

Can I give a discount or run a sale?

Yes, you can give a discount on any bill today. Season sale offers, such as a percent off or buy 2 get 1 on chosen dates, are coming soon.

Can salesmen get commission?

Yes, on the Enterprise and Custom plans. Set commission rules for each salesman and see the commission report. Each salesman also gets their own login, so every bill shows who sold it.

I also stitch clothes. Is this the right software?

This page is for shops that sell ready-made and unstitched clothes. If most of your work is stitching orders with customer sizes, see our tailor shop POS software.
