The problems

Common problems vape and pan shops face without POS software

A vape or pan shop sells many small items at speed. One e-liquid comes in many flavours and bottle sizes, coils come in packs of five, and the evening rush brings ten customers at once. A diary and a calculator cannot keep up. Good vape shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    Too many flavours to count

    One e-liquid comes in eight flavours and three bottle sizes. You do not know which flavour is finished until a customer asks for it.

    The variant generator makes every flavour and size in one go, each with its own stock and price, and low stock alerts warn you early.

  • The problem

    Packs and pieces mixed up

    You buy coils in a pack of five, but one customer wants one coil and another wants two packs. The stock count turns into a guess.

    Units: sell by the pack or by the piece, box or piece. Stock is always kept in the unit you sell.

  • The problem

    Cash short at closing

    Two staff work the counter in the evening. At night the drawer is short and nobody knows which shift it was.

    Cash sessions: each shift opens and closes its own drawer, with staff logins and cashier records.

  • The problem

    Slow bills in the rush

    Customers wait while you look for the price of a pod or a pack of supari. Some walk away.

    Scan the barcode or search by name. Quick bill, hold a bill, and serve the next customer.

  • The problem

    Supplier balance unclear

    You take stock from many suppliers. Nobody knows what is still due to each one and which bill is old.

    Purchase invoice, goods received (GRN) and a supplier ledger with the balance due and how old each bill is.

  • The problem

    Load-shedding stops the counter

    The light goes, the internet goes, and customers are waiting at the counter with cash in hand.

    Our vape shop POS software keeps cash bills running on the Windows and Android apps and syncs when the internet is back.

How it works

How vape shop POS software works, from purchase to profit

This is the full cycle of a vape or pan shop in UpTrendPos vape shop POS software. Each step feeds the next: when you save a bill, the stock, the cash in the drawer and your accounts change on their own.

  1. Receive stockEnter the purchase from your supplier. Packs, boxes and bottles go in; stock and average cost update.
  2. Open the shiftThe cashier logs in and opens a cash session with the opening cash in the drawer.
  3. Bill the customerScan or search, add packs, pieces and bottles, and print or send the bill.
  4. Close the shiftCount the drawer and close the session. Any short or extra cash shows at once.
  5. See your profitSales, cost and expenses go into your accounts on their own. Check the day report at night.

Can I make every flavour and bottle size of one e-liquid?

Yes. The variant generator takes two lists, for example flavours and bottle sizes, and makes every item in one go. Each item has its own code, stock and price. Devices can use model and colour the same way.

Can I sell coils and pods by the pack and by the piece?

Yes. Set the units for each item once. Sell a full pack or a single piece, and stock always stays in the selling unit. Supari and mouth fresheners can sell by the box or piece, and cold drinks by the crate or bottle.

How do cash sessions work?

Each cashier logs in and opens a cash session with the opening cash. Every bill is recorded against that cashier. At the end of the shift, count the drawer and close the session; any short or extra cash shows at once.

No. UpTrendPos does not check age. If your shop sells some items only to adults (18+), that is your shop's own policy, and your staff follow it at the counter.

Can regular customers buy on udhaar?

Yes. An unpaid sale goes on the customer ledger with every payment. Send the statement as PDF, print or WhatsApp. Udhaar needs internet so every device shows the same balance.
