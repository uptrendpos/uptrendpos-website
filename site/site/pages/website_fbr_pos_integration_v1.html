Why it matters

What happens without FBR POS integration

If you are a Tier-1 retailer, FBR POS integration is required by the Sales Tax Act, 1990. These are the costs of waiting.

  • Rs 500,000Penalty for the first defaultIt grows to Rs 1 million, Rs 2 million and Rs 3 million for later defaults.
  • 60%Less input taxA Tier-1 retailer who is not integrated loses 60% of adjustable input tax for that period.
  • SealedShop and utilitiesFBR can seal the shop and ask to cut gas and electricity connections.
  • 24 hoursFor offline billsBills made during an outage must reach FBR within 24 hours after the system is back.

Source: Sales Tax Act 1990 sections 3(9A), 8B(6), 14AB and 33, and the Sales Tax Rules, 2006 (Chapter XIV). Rules change, so always check the latest on the FBR website. This is general guidance, not tax advice.

How to get FBR POS integration

FBR POS integration in 5 simple steps

Most of the work is your FBR registration. The UpTrendPos side takes a few minutes, and our team does it with you on WhatsApp.

  1. Check if you are Tier-1Use the checker above. Any one test is enough.
  2. Register in IRISGet your NTN and STRN on FBR's IRIS portal. Declare your outlet and register every counter that takes payment.
  3. Get your FBR POS detailsFBR gives each counter a POS registration number and terminal ID, and you get your API token. FBR rules say integration runs through a licensed integrator, and PRAL offers this service free of cost.
  4. Add them to UpTrendPosOpen Settings, FBR Fiscalization. Enter your POS registration number, NTN, STRN, terminal ID and token. Test a few bills in Sandbox.
  5. Go liveSwitch the mode to Production and turn FBR on. From now on, every bill carries the FBR invoice number, QR code and Re 1 fee.

What prints on an FBR invoice from UpTrendPos

FBR asks for a set format on every bill from an integrated counter. UpTrendPos prints all of it on thermal receipts and A4 invoices:

  • Shop name, NTN and STRN
  • POS ID of the counter
  • Your own invoice number, date and time
  • Each item with quantity and price
  • Value without tax, sales tax rate and amount
  • The Re 1 FBR POS fee
  • The FBR invoice number, in the format FBR gives (for example 123456-141026103512-0001)
  • A QR code made from the FBR invoice number, so buyers can verify the bill

Load-shedding

FBR POS integration during load-shedding

Light and internet go off in every city. FBR rules allow for it: bills made during an outage must be marked as offline and sent to FBR within 24 hours after the system is back. If a shop stays cut off from FBR for too long, it can face action.

  • Billing keeps running offline on Windows and Android
  • Offline bills are kept safe on the device
  • They are reported to FBR when the internet returns
  • Connect your counters as soon as the internet is back
  • Keep a UPS for the router so short power cuts do not stop reporting

POS integration vs digital invoicing

FBR POS integration is not the same as digital invoicing

People mix these two up. Here is the simple difference.

FBR POS integration

  • For Tier-1 retailers selling at the counter
  • Every sale is reported to FBR as it happens
  • Bill shows FBR invoice number, QR and Re 1 fee
  • Under section 3(9A) of the Sales Tax Act
  • This is what UpTrendPos does today

Digital invoicing

  • For sales tax registered businesses, mostly business-to-business
  • Invoices to other registered buyers are sent to FBR
  • Separate rules under section 23 of the Sales Tax Act
  • Meeting one does not cover the other
  • Ask us if your business needs both

FAQ

FBR POS integration questions

What is FBR POS integration?

FBR POS integration means your POS sends every sale to FBR as it happens. FBR sends back an invoice number, and your bill prints it with a QR code. It is required for Tier-1 retailers under the Sales Tax Act, 1990, so FBR can see retail sales in real time.

Who must get FBR POS integration?

Tier-1 retailers must integrate. You are Tier-1 if any one test applies: part of a chain, shop in an air-conditioned mall, electricity bills over Rs 1.2 million a year, wholesaler-cum-retailer, card machine at the counter, high 236G or 236H tax, or notified by FBR.

How do I get FBR POS integration with UpTrendPos?

Register in IRIS and get your POS details from FBR for each counter. Then enter them in UpTrendPos under FBR settings, test in Sandbox and switch to Production. Our team does the UpTrendPos part with you on WhatsApp and checks your first live bill.

What is the Re 1 POS fee?

It is a service fee of one rupee on every invoice from an FBR-integrated counter. The buyer pays it, it is shown on the bill, and you pay it to FBR with your monthly sales tax return. UpTrendPos adds it to each FBR bill on its own.

What happens if internet or light goes off?

You keep billing. Offline bills are kept on the device and reported to FBR when the internet is back. FBR rules say offline bills must reach FBR within 24 hours after the system is restored, so reconnect your counters as soon as you can.

What is the penalty for not integrating?

The penalty is Rs 500,000 for the first default, then Rs 1 million, Rs 2 million and Rs 3 million for later defaults. A non-integrated Tier-1 retailer also loses 60% of adjustable input tax, and FBR can seal the shop. Check the latest rules on fbr.gov.pk.

Is UpTrendPos approved by FBR?

UpTrendPos is POS software with FBR POS integration built in. It uses the official FBR system with the details FBR gives you, and FBR rules route integration through a licensed integrator such as PRAL. We do not claim to be FBR or an FBR department.

How can my customer check an FBR bill?

The customer scans the QR code on the bill with FBR's Tax Asaan app. The app shows whether the invoice was reported to FBR. This builds trust, because the buyer can see that the sales tax on the bill is real and recorded.

How much does FBR POS integration cost?

It is included in the UpTrendPos Enterprise and Custom plans. FBR does not charge for integration itself, and PRAL offers its integration service free. See the pricing page for plan prices and a 14-day free trial.

Can I use FBR on more than one counter or branch?

Yes. Register every counter in IRIS, then add each counter's FBR details in UpTrendPos. Each branch keeps its own stock and FBR setup, and the owner sees all branches from one account. Branch and user limits depend on your plan.

I am not Tier-1. Do I need this?

Not for the Tier-1 rule. But if you are registered for sales tax, other FBR rules such as digital invoicing may apply to you. You can also turn on the Local GST line to show sales tax on bills without FBR reporting. Ask us what fits your shop.
