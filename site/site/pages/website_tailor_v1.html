The problems

Common problems tailors face without POS software

A tailor shop runs on sizes, dates and promises. A paper register cannot keep up when Eid orders pile in. Good tailor shop POS software fixes these problems first.

  • The problem

    The size register gets lost

    A torn page or a lost register means every regular customer has to be measured again.

    Every size is saved on the customer's ID and phone number, for every next order.

  • The problem

    Delivery dates are missed

    In the Eid rush, nobody remembers which suit is due today and which is due next week.

    Every order has a delivery date and a status, from booked to delivered.

  • The problem

    Advance fights at the counter

    The advance was written on a slip that the customer lost, and now the balance is in doubt.

    Advance is saved at booking. The balance shows on the order and the slip.

  • The problem

    Who is cutting this suit?

    The cutting master and the stitching master pass orders by word of mouth, and suits get stuck.

    Assign a cutting master and a stitching master from your staff on every order.

  • The problem

    Sizes change, records do not

    A customer gains weight or wants a looser fit, and the old size is used by mistake.

    Update the size on the next order. The new size is saved for every order after it.

  • The problem

    Old customers, new system

    Moving to software feels risky when every customer knows his old register number.

    Type the old register number as the customer ID, like 1283, and keep going.

How it works

How tailor shop POS software works, from size to delivery

This is the full cycle of a stitching order in UpTrendPos tailor shop POS software. Each step feeds the next, so orders, payments and accounts always match.

  1. Take the sizeFind the customer by ID or phone, or add a new one. Save or update his size.
  2. Book the orderAdd the suits, the stitching rate and the delivery date. Take the advance and give the order slip.

FAQ

Tailor shop POS software questions

Which is the best tailor shop POS software in Pakistan?

The best tailor shop POS software is the one built for stitching orders, not just shelf sales. Check five things: sizes saved by customer, delivery dates, order status, advance and balance, and a workshop slip. UpTrendPos is tailoring software that does all five, and you can test it free for 14 days.

Can I keep my old register numbers?

Yes. When you add a customer, you can type his old register number as his customer ID, for example 1283. He keeps the same number he knows, and you can search by it, by name or by phone.

Which sizes can I save?

You can save sizes for shalwar kameez, coat, waistcoat and more on each customer. The sizes stay on his record and open on every new order, so you do not measure a regular customer again.

What if a customer's size changes?

Just update the size on his next order. The new size is saved on the customer and used for every order after it, so the old size is never used by mistake.

How does this tailor shop software track orders?

Every stitching order has a delivery date and a status: booked, cutting, stitching, ready and delivered. You can see where each suit is and who is working on it at any time.

Can I assign orders to my karigars?

Yes. Pick a cutting master and a stitching master from your staff on each order. Karigars are kept as staff in HRM, and attendance and salary come with the Custom plan.

How do I handle advance and balance?

Take the advance when you book the order. The balance shows on the order and the slip. When the customer collects his suit, take the balance and give or send the receipt.

Can I send the order slip on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send the order slip on WhatsApp, or print it on a thermal printer or on A4 paper. A separate size slip can also be printed for your workshop, like a kitchen print in a restaurant.

Will my customers get a message when the suit is ready?

Our customer app is coming soon. With it, your customer will get a notification at every status of his order, from booked to ready. Until then, you can send the slip and receipt on WhatsApp.

Can I also sell ready-made suits and fabric?
