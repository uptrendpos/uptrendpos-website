FAQ

Questions about industry-specific POS software

What is industry-specific POS software?

It is POS software that is set up for one kind of business. A pharmacy gets batch and expiry, a clothing shop gets size and colour, and a kiryana store gets kg and pack billing. You get the fields your trade needs and do not fill the ones it does not.

Which business types does UpTrendPos support?

UpTrendPos supports 30+ business types in Pakistan. These include grocery, pharmacy, clothing, shoes, tailoring, cosmetics, electronics, solar, mobile, hardware, paint, auto parts, jewellery and more. Pick your type when you sign up and your shop opens ready for it.

Can I change my business type later?

Ask our team before you change it. Your sales, stock and udhaar stay in your account, but some product fields depend on the business type. Message us on WhatsApp and we will guide you to the right setup, step by step, so your data stays clean.

My shop sells two kinds of things. What should I pick?

Pick the type that makes most of your sales. For example, a general store that also sells some medicines can pick Grocery and Kiryana. You can turn extra product fields on or off in settings, and our team helps you, so one account covers your whole shop.

Does every business type work offline?

Yes. Billing works without internet on the Windows and Android apps for every business type. Your sales are saved on the device and sync to your account on their own when the internet comes back, with no double entries.

How much does UpTrendPos cost?

The price depends on your plan, not on your business type. Every plan works for all 30+ types, and plans differ by branches, users and some advanced features. See the details on our POS software pricing page or start a 14-day free trial.
